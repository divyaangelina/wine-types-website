Types of Wine

A simple guide to some of the most popular wine categories, their flavor profiles, and what makes each one unique.

🍷 🍇 🍾 ✨

A Quick Introduction

Wine can be grouped by color, sweetness, body, and production method. While there are many grape varieties and regional styles, most wines fall into a few broad categories: red, white, rosé, sparkling, and dessert wines. Each offers a different tasting experience and pairs well with different foods.

Main Wine Categories

🍷

Red Wine

Made from dark-skinned grapes and fermented with the grape skins, giving red wine its deep color and tannic structure.

Examples: Cabernet Sauvignon, Pinot Noir, Merlot, Syrah
🥂

White Wine

Usually made without extended skin contact, resulting in lighter color and often brighter acidity and fruit-forward flavors.

Examples: Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling, Pinot Grigio
🌸

Rosé Wine

Produced from red grapes with only brief skin contact, giving it a pink hue and a fresh, crisp style between red and white wine.

Examples: Provence Rosé, White Zinfandel, Rosado
🍾

Sparkling Wine

Known for its bubbles, sparkling wine undergoes a second fermentation that creates carbonation and lively texture.

Examples: Champagne, Prosecco, Cava, Crémant
🍯

Dessert Wine

Typically sweeter and often served in smaller portions, dessert wines are crafted to highlight concentrated sugar and rich flavor.

Examples: Port, Sauternes, Ice Wine, Moscato d'Asti
🧀

Fortified Wine

Fortified wines have a distilled spirit added during production, increasing alcohol content and often contributing complexity and sweetness.

Examples: Sherry, Port, Madeira, Marsala

Body & Texture

Wines are often described as light-bodied, medium-bodied, or full-bodied depending on how heavy and rich they feel on the palate.

Sweetness Levels

Wines can range from bone dry to very sweet. Sweetness depends on how much residual sugar remains after fermentation.

Food Pairing Tip

A simple rule: pair lighter wines with lighter foods and fuller wines with richer, heartier dishes.
